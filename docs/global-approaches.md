# How other countries handle inheritance in land records

The six cases that matter most for us. We read summaries and official pages; check full texts before quoting exact numbers.

| Country | What they did | What happened | What we take |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Japan** | From 1 April 2024, heirs must register an inheritance within 3 years; fine up to 100,000 yen. If heirs haven't agreed how to divide the land, they can first file a simpler heir declaration ([Hiroshima City](https://www.city.hiroshima.lg.jp/english/paperwork/1029827/1009917.html), [Real Estate Tokyo](https://www.realestate-tokyo.com/news/inheritance-registration-mandatory)) | Made law because unregistered inheritances had left land with unknown owners estimated larger than Kyushu ([Real Estate Tokyo](https://www.realestate-tokyo.com/news/real-estate-guide/amendment-of-japan-s-real-property-registration-act)) | Same problem as Karnataka. Japan fixed it with a deadline plus a simple first filing; we make that first filing easy, even before the family agrees on division |
| **United States** | "Heirs' property": land passed down without a clear legal heir record. The Uniform Partition of Heirs Property Act (2010) protects heirs from forced sales, adopted in 21 states by 2024 ([Cambridge](https://www.cambridge.org/core/product/D90A1CBE535C040CFF2A267423FE3A0F), [Atlanta Fed](https://www.atlantafed.org/community-development/publications/partners-update/2020/covid-19-publications/201007-expansion-of-new-law-in-southeast-may-stave-off-black-land-loss)) | Cited as a leading cause of Black farmland loss; heirs' property is cut off from federal loans and grants ([Union of Concerned Scientists](https://ucsusa.org/resources/lost-inheritance)) | Unclear heirs cost families land and credit. Settle heirs early and on the record |
| **China** | Nationwide confirmation and registration of rural land contract rights: piloted 2009, rolled out from 2013 ([PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9451968)) | Positive effect on long-term land investment ([Zhou, Cheng, Zhang](https://agris.fao.org/search/en/records/65de2c2c90674e46e6530ef9)); effects on renting land are mixed ([Land Use Policy 2020](https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/lauspo/v99y2020ics026483771930599x.html)) | Registered rights change how farmers invest. Scale needs a state-backed process, so we feed the government's, not a parallel one |
| **Estonia** | A notary certifies the heirs once; the heir applies to the land register online with a digital signature; entering the heir is free of state fees ([EU e-Justice](https://e-justice.europa.eu/topics/family-matters-inheritance/inheritance/information-authorities-issuing-ecs/ee_en)) | A short, digital path from death to updated record | One heir decision, reused for the application. Remove the fee and the trips |
| **Rwanda** | Registered about 8.4 million parcels in three years through a low-cost, participatory programme ([World Bank](https://openknowledge.worldbank.org/server/api/core/bitstreams/d63797f6-e70f-54b9-8dd8-6ab36c658793/content)) | Inheritance rights recorded without gender bias in the pilot ([Ali, Deininger, Goldstein](https://wider.unu.edu/publication/environmental-and-gender-impacts-land-tenure-regularization-africa)), but later transfers mostly stayed unregistered | A one-time drive isn't enough. Updating has to stay cheap and local, which is our role after Karnataka's campaign |
| **Ethiopia** | Low-cost certification with land certificates in both spouses' names | Joint certificates raised women's empowerment ([Melesse, Dabissa, Bulte 2018](https://ideas.repec.org/a/taf/jdevst/v54y2018i10p1756-1774.html)) | Every heir, wives and daughters included, goes on the record |

## The best approach, combined

1. **Make the first step simple** (Japan's heir declaration): the family can start even before everyone agrees on dividing the land.
2. **One certified heir decision** (Estonia), produced by tested rules and matching the official family tree certificate.
3. **Everyone on the record**, women included (Ethiopia; Deininger et al. on Karnataka's own 1994 reform).
4. **Cheap, local and ongoing**, not a one-time drive (Rwanda): voice on a phone, or an operator at a service centre.
5. **Inside the government process**, not beside it (China's state-backed rollout).
