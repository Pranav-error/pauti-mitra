# Research

We read summaries and excerpts of these; numbers here are as reported by each source.

## Clear title and access to credit

| Source | What it says |
|---|---|
| [NCAER: land as collateral for institutional credit](https://ncaer.org/?p=14819) (Prabhakar, Sanan, Sen) | Clear title favours loans; land as collateral is a frequent lender requirement (six-state evidence). |
| [Chakraborty et al.: land as collateral in India](https://judhajitc.github.io/files/LandascollateralinIndia.pdf) | Cheap information on ownership and past encumbrances lets lenders judge whether a parcel can be collateral. |
| [AERC Study 194](https://www.visvabharati.ac.in/home/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/AERC_Study-194_02.pdf) | Clear, legally recognised titles raise farmers' investment and access to institutional credit (West Bengal). |
| [EAC-PM: SVAMITVA](https://eacpm.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Unlocking-property-rights-through-SVAMITVA-Fin-rev.pdf) | Formal property rights helped women and lower-income groups use property as collateral. |
| [ISEC Policy Brief 59](https://www.isec.ac.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/PB-59-Strengthening-Land-Titling-to-Enhance-Farmers-Final.pdf) | Households with unclear titles received fewer benefits from government schemes. |
| Thomas & Uday, xKDR (2021) | Only a weak link between record quality alone and formal borrowing. Records are necessary, not sufficient. |

## Land records and titling in India

| Source | What it says |
|---|---|
| [PRS: Land Records and Titles in India](https://prsindia.org/policy/analytical-reports/land-records-and-titles-india) | Presumptive titling, buyer bears the checking; 47% of mutations computerised vs 86% of records. |
| [Vidhi: Is land digitisation enough?](https://vidhilegalpolicy.in/blog/is-land-digitisation-enough/) | Digitisation alone hasn't addressed ownership problems. |
| [Lok Sabha RD&PR committee report 41](https://sansad.in/getFile/app/lsscommittee/Rural%20Development%20and%20Panchayati%20Raj/18_Rural_Development_and_Panchayati_Raj_41.pdf?source=app) | Inheritance, partition and court-order changes not reflected promptly. |
| [Carnegie: Delhi land litigation study](https://carnegieendowment.org/events/2021/09/land-litigation-in-india-learnings-from-a-study-on-land-litigation-in-delhi) | Record-related disputes are 13.6% of property litigation at the Delhi HC. |
